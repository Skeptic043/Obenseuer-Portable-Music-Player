# Changelog

## Version 0.2.4

Initial beta release.

- Pocket Music Player runs on AA batteries and the Rechargeable Music Player can be charged at any charger
- Budget Earbuds, Work Headphones and Hi-Fi Headphones, each with their own sound quality
- MP3, OGG, WAV and FLAC support, plus M4A and AAC where Windows Media Foundation is available
- Support for custom folder locations
- Support for Obentunes
- Players and headphones turn up in loot and at traders
